BusBoard NL is a focused transit companion for Metrobus riders in St. John's, Mount Pearl, and Paradise.

Two tabs keep it simple: the Board for your stop's next buses, and the Map for everything else.

Check your stop on the Board

• See your stop's live departures in a list, with no map in the way.
• Keep your stops as chips at the top and switch between them in a tap, or pick Near me.
• Enter a four-digit stop ID, or choose a nearby stop and see where each route goes from it.
• Compare Metrobus live predictions with scheduled departures.
• At night, see when the first bus comes.
• Set an arrival alert: get a notification when your bus should be 3, 5 or 10 minutes away.
• Pull to refresh for the latest available information.

See every bus on the Map

• View current bus locations on an interactive map, with smooth movement between Metrobus position updates.
• Tap any stop for its live departures, right on the map.
• Follow a bus to your stop: tap Follow on a live bus to see its route, the stops left, and when it arrives.
• Filter the map by route, then tap a bus for its vehicle and trip details.

Plan a trip

• Tap "Where to?" and plan to a stop, your location, any spot on the map, or a saved place like Home or Work.
• Leave now, leave by or arrive by any minute you pick.
• Save the trips you take often and see their next buses at a glance.
• Plans use live bus times for the next hour, all worked out on your phone.

Stay informed

• Read current Metrobus service alerts and route or stop impacts.
• See alerts that apply to the stop or bus you are viewing.
• Use light or dark mode. Motion stays calm, and turns off when your phone's Reduce Motion setting is on.

BusBoard NL uses Metrobus public schedule, GTFS-Realtime, and stop-prediction data. Vehicle positions and predictions update periodically and can be delayed or unavailable. Please be at your stop early.

BusBoard NL is an independent app and is not affiliated with Metrobus Transit.
