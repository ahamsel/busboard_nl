BusBoard NL is a focused transit companion for Metrobus riders in St. John's, Mount Pearl, and Paradise.

See what is happening now

• View current bus locations on an interactive map.
• Track each physical bus separately, with smooth movement between Metrobus position updates.
• Follow a bus to your stop: see its route, the stops left, and when it arrives.
• Filter the map by route, then tap a bus for its vehicle and trip details.

Plan from any stop

• Enter a four-digit stop ID or choose a nearby stop.
• Compare Metrobus live predictions with scheduled departures.
• Set an arrival alert: get a notification when your bus is 3, 5 or 10 minutes away.
• See run and vehicle details when Metrobus provides them.
• Pull to refresh for the latest available information.

Stay informed

• Read current Metrobus service alerts and route or stop impacts.
• See alerts that apply to the stop or bus you are viewing.
• Use light or dark mode with a clean interface built for quick checks.

BusBoard NL uses Metrobus public schedule, GTFS-Realtime, and stop-prediction data. Vehicle positions and predictions update periodically and can be delayed or unavailable. Please be at your stop early.

BusBoard NL is an independent app and is not affiliated with Metrobus Transit.
