New in 1.3.0:
• Arrival alerts: tap the bell on an arrival, choose 3, 5 or 10 minutes, and BusBoard NL lets you know when your bus is about that far away
• While an alert is set, a quiet notification counts down with the live prediction; tap it to open your stop
• Cancel an alert from your stop's board or from the notification
• Alerts follow Metrobus live predictions, which can be off by a minute or two
