New in 1.4.0:
• Trip planner: the new Plan tab plans a trip leaving now, leaving by or arriving by any minute you pick
• Start and end at a stop, your location or any spot on the map; press and hold the map to plan from or to it
• Save trips you take often and see their next buses on the Plan tab
• Plans use live bus times for the next hour, all worked out on your phone
• Tap a live bus on a stop's card on the map to follow it
• Late buses keep their live times past midnight
