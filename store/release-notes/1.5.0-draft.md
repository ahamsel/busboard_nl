## App Store: What's new (en-CA)

New in 1.5.0:
- Two tabs. The Board shows your stop's live buses with no map in the way. You asked for it! The Map is for planning trips and following buses
- A brighter blue look, cleaner greys and a new icon
- Tap Follow on any live bus to watch it come to you on the map
- Tap "Where to?" to plan a trip to a stop, a spot on the map or a saved place like Home or Work
- On the map, "Where to?" and your stop's next bus sit in a slim bar that tucks away as you pan
- Choose a day and time with BusBoard's own picker, with quick picks like In 15 min
- Smooth motion throughout, and none of it when Reduce Motion is on
- Trip names read normally instead of in ALL CAPS
- At night, your stop shows when the first bus comes
- A roomier layout on iPad

## App Store: promotional text (141 of 170 characters)

A fresh look with two tabs: the Board shows your stop's live buses, no map needed, and the Map plans trips and follows your bus to your stop.

## Privacy policy line (portfolio site, once 1.5.0 is live)

Saved places and the stops you viewed recently are stored only on your device, like saved trips.
