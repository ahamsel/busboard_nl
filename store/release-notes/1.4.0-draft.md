## Google Play: What's new (en-US)

New in 1.4.0:
• Trip planner: the new Plan tab works out a trip from one place to another, leaving now, leaving by or arriving by any minute you pick
• Start and end at a stop, by number or name, at your location, or at any spot you pick on the map; press and hold the map to plan from or to that spot
• Save the trips you take often and see their next buses right on the Plan tab
• Plans use live bus times for the next hour
• Everything is worked out on your phone
